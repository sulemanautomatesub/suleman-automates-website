How it works

From incoming call to qualified enquiry

The assistant follows a clear workflow on every call — and you decide what it says, what it collects, and when your team takes over.

Answers incoming calls

Buyer and tenant calls are answered on your enquiry line — day, night and weekends.

Answers common questions

Responds to frequent questions using the information your agency agrees on in advance.

Captures requirements

Location, property type, purpose, budget and timeline — collected during a natural conversation.

Passes details to your team

Each enquiry reaches your agents with a structured summary, ready for follow-up.

Where it helps

Everyday enquiries, handled consistently

Buyer & tenant enquiries

First-time questions about listings, areas and availability get an immediate, consistent answer — and every caller is qualified.

After-hours & overflow

Calls that arrive outside office hours — or while your team is busy — are still answered and captured instead of going to voicemail.

Consistent data for agents

Your team receives enquiries in the same structured format every time, so follow-ups start with the full picture.
