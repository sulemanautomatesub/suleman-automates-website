Call workflow

Three steps, every enquiry

Step 1 · Caller

The call comes in

A buyer or tenant calls your enquiry line — during office hours, after hours, or while your team is busy with viewings.

  • Answered immediately, every time
  • Your agency's greeting and tone
  • No hold music, no voicemail dead-ends
Step 2 · Assistant

Questions answered, requirements captured

The assistant responds to common questions using the information your agency agreed on, then collects what the caller is actually looking for.

  • Area, property type, purpose (buy or rent)
  • Budget range and timeline
  • Contact details for follow-up
Step 3 · Your team

Enquiry passed to the agency

Each enquiry arrives with a structured summary, so your agents start the follow-up with the full picture instead of a missed-call notification.

  • Structured enquiry summary
  • Delivered to your team's workflow
  • Basic CRM integration included

Common questions

Straight answers on scope

Which CRMs does the assistant integrate with?

Basic CRM integration is included, subject to your compatible setup and the agreed scope. If your CRM needs complex or custom work, we discuss that as a separate custom setup before anything is built.

Can the assistant handle WhatsApp messages too?

WhatsApp message handling is a custom requirement for discussion — it is not part of the standard packages and is not presented as a live feature today. If it matters to your agency, tell us on WhatsApp and we'll scope it.

How does the assistant know what to say?

You agree the information the assistant may use — listings overview, common questions, service details — and packages include monthly FAQ updates so the answers stay current with your agreed scope.

When does my team take over a call?

The workflow is designed so the assistant captures the enquiry and passes it to your team with a structured summary. Handover behaviour — for example when a caller needs something outside the agreed scope — is defined together during setup.
